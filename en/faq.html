Getting started

Q1How much does a project cost?

It depends on the scope: a landing site is not an e-commerce platform. After one free consultation you get a fixed quote with a clear scope. The price you approve is the price you pay.

Article: Website development cost in Egypt 2026

Q2How long does it take?

A typical first version ships in 4 to 8 weeks depending on scope, with a working link you can see every week from week one, as described in how we work.

Q3I have an idea but I'm not technical. Where do I start?

That is exactly what the free consultation is for. Tell us the business goal in your own words, and we translate it into what is possible, what it costs and how long it takes.

Article: How to choose a software companyAsk us on WhatsApp

Q4What do you need from me to get started?

Three things: describe your goal in plain words, share examples you like (any site or app), and be available for quick decisions when we ask. We handle design, content structure and everything technical. Send us your idea.

Ownership & security

Q5Who owns the code?

You do, 100%. Code, design and data are yours, delivered with documentation. There is no lock-in: any developer can continue the work after us.

Q6My idea is confidential. How is it protected?

We sign an NDA before you share any details. It is standard practice for us. Your idea, data and business information stay confidential whether or not we end up working together.

Q7Who pays for hosting and running costs?

Hosting and domain are registered in your name and stay yours. Typical costs are known upfront with no hidden markup. We set everything up and can manage it for you through our hosting and operations service.

Payment & contract

Q8Do I pay everything upfront?

No. Payment is split into installments tied to delivery milestones you can see and test. You pay for progress you have verified, not for promises.

Q9Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes. We work fully remotely in Arabic and English, and our platforms ship bilingual when your market needs it. See our work.

After launch

Q10What happens after launch?

We don't disappear. Support and maintenance plans keep your platform fast, secure and up to date, and we are there for the next feature when you are ready.

Q11I already have an old website or system. Rebuild or improve?

We assess it first. Sometimes targeted improvements get you 80% of the value at a fraction of the cost; sometimes a rebuild is genuinely cheaper in the long run. We tell you which, with reasons, before you spend.
