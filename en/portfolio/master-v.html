Overview

A real estate data platform for brokers in Egypt's new cities. It gives agents a daily-updated database of compounds, units, prices and full payment plans, with one-tap WhatsApp sending, an investment ROI calculator and commission tracking with tax breakdowns. Mobile apps are on their way to the stores.

The result

10K+ brokers active on the platform across 1K+ compounds and 15K+ live-priced units, with data refreshed every day.

What the platform delivers

  • Daily-updated database of compounds, phases, units and prices
  • Complete payment plans: down payment, installments, delivery and fees
  • Send brochures, prices and plans to clients on WhatsApp in one tap
  • Investment ROI calculator: CAP rate, IRR, NPV and payback period
  • Commission tracker from claim to collection, with taxes calculated automatically
  • Team accounts: sub-users scoped by city and section
