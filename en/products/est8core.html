Overview

Est8Core is a CRM built specifically for real estate brokerages and individual brokers. Leads are captured and assigned automatically, properties are managed as branch inventory with public listing pages, and deals close through a real workflow: commission splits between agency and agent, installment schedules and manager approvals above your thresholds. All of it is multi-tenant with a full team hierarchy.

Who is it for?Real estate brokerage companies (branches, teams and roles) and individual brokers.

What it delivers

  • Lead capture with automatic assignment and follow-up tracking
  • Deal workflow: commission split, co-broke and agent share, calculated automatically on close
  • Installment schedules per deal with paid tracking and alerts
  • Manager approval gates for deals above your thresholds
  • Property inventory per branch and public listing pages with clean URLs
  • Full hierarchy: branches, teams and granular role permissions
  • Real-time notifications the moment a lead or deal moves
