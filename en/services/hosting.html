Services

Hosting & Operations

We set up and run your infrastructure: servers, automated deployments, monitoring and backups. Your platform stays online, updates ship in minutes and you know what it costs every month.

01What we build
  • 01

    Server setup

    A secured VPS or cloud setup sized for your traffic, registered in your name.

  • 02

    Automated deployments

    Every approved change goes live through GitHub Actions in minutes, with a quick rollback.

  • 03

    Monitoring and alerts

    Uptime checks and performance dashboards that alert us before your customers notice.

  • 04

    Backups and recovery

    Scheduled backups of databases and files to separate storage, tested for restore.

  • 05

    Speed and cost tuning

    Caching, CDN and right-sized servers so you stop paying for capacity you do not use.

  • 06

    Platform migration

    Move from shared hosting or another provider with no data loss and minimal downtime.

05Questions about this service
VPS or cloud?+

A well-run VPS covers most projects at a fraction of cloud cost. We move to AWS, Google Cloud or Azure when scale or your company policy requires it.

Who owns the servers?+

You do. Hosting and domain are registered in your name, and we manage them for you.

What happens if the site goes down at night?+

Monitoring alerts us within minutes, and tested backups let us restore service quickly.
