Mobile Apps

We build apps for iPhone and Android from one codebase with Flutter or React Native, connect them to your systems and publish them on both stores under your name.

  • 03

    Targeted push notifications that bring users back at the right moment.

05Questions about this service
Flutter or React Native?+

We choose per project: Flutter for highly custom interfaces, React Native when you already run a React web stack and want to share logic.

Do I need two separate apps?+

No. One codebase serves both iPhone and Android, which lowers cost and keeps both versions in sync.

Who publishes the app on the stores?+

We do, under your own developer accounts, so the app always stays in your name.
